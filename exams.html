PPSC One-Paper Tests

Paper Pattern

  • 100 MCQs in a single paper (commonly around 90 minutes).
  • +1 mark for each correct answer, −0.25 for each wrong answer; skipped questions score 0.
  • Typical mix: English, General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies, Islamiat, Everyday Science, Current Affairs, basic Mathematics and Computer — proportions vary by post.
  • Shortlisted candidates are called for interview; final merit combines written + interview marks.

Eligibility (General)

  • Education and age limits are set per post in each advertisement — most posts require at least a bachelor's degree.
  • Domicile of Punjab is generally required for PPSC posts.
  • Age relaxation rules (for government servants, minorities, etc.) are announced in the advertisement.

PMA Long Course — Pakistan Army

Selection Stages

  1. Initial test at Army Selection & Recruitment Centres (AS&RCs) — written / intelligence test plus academic MCQs (English, Maths, Islamiat, Pak Studies, General Knowledge).
  2. ISSB — 4–5 days of psychological, group and interview testing.
  3. Medical examination and final selection on merit.
  4. Training at the Pakistan Military Academy, Kakul (two-year course).

Eligibility (General)

  • Unmarried male citizens of Pakistan.
  • Age generally 17–22 years for intermediate-qualified candidates, with a higher limit for graduates.
  • Minimum education: FA / FSc or equivalent; higher qualifications are accepted per the advertisement.
  • Physical and medical standards apply as per the induction rules.

PAF & Pakistan Navy Initial Tests

✈️ Pakistan Air Force

  • Courses include GD(P) (General Duty Pilot), Aeronautical Engineering, Air Defence, Admin & Special Duties, Logistics and others — each with its own education requirement.
  • Initial test: intelligence test plus academic test (English, Physics, Maths as per course), followed by ISSB / interviews and medical.
  • GD(P) generally requires FSc (Pre-Engineering / Pre-Medical / Computer Science) — confirm per course in the advertisement.

⚓ Pakistan Navy — PN Cadet

  • PN Cadet course: initial intelligence + academic test (English, Maths, Physics, General Knowledge), then ISSB, medical and final selection.
  • Training at the Pakistan Naval Academy; branches include Operations, Marine Engineering, Supply and others.
  • Education and age criteria are announced per course — typically FSc (Pre-Engineering) for PN Cadet.

Common Questions

Is negative marking the same in every PPSC paper?

No — it is announced per advertisement, but the standard one-paper rule is −0.25 per wrong answer. This portal trains you on that standard rule; always read the specific advertisement.

Do Armed Forces initial tests have negative marking?

No. The initial written/intelligence tests of the Army, PAF and Navy do not carry negative marking — so attempting every question is the right strategy. That is why Armed Forces mode on this portal has no penalty.

Can MCQ practice help for CSS, which is a descriptive exam?

Yes — for the compulsory papers. Current Affairs, Pakistan Affairs, General Science & Ability and Islamiat all reward the same factual base that MCQs drill. Essay and précis need separate written practice.

Are the eligibility details on this page final?

No. Age limits, education requirements and course details change with each induction cycle. This page follows the widely published patterns; the official advertisement is always final.
