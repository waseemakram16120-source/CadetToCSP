How the ranking works

  • Total Score — the sum of your net scores across all tests (PPSC −0.25 negative marking applies).
  • Stars — earned per test: 3 stars at 90%+ accuracy, 2 at 70%+, 1 at 50%+, otherwise 0.
  • Best — your best accuracy in any single test.
  • Streak — consecutive days with at least one test.
  • The board refreshes after every finished test. Opted out? Your row disappears — turn it back on from the Dashboard.
